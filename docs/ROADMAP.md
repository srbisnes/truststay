# TrustStay 18-Month Roadmap

## Months 1–3 — Accelerate
Five specialist contributors. Core product flows. Authentication. Wallets. Listings. Search. Booking. Escrow. USDC. Reputation foundation. AI foundation. Testing. Deployment.

Exit criteria: advanced functional beta candidate with documented limitations and reproducible build/test process.

## Months 4–6 — Private Beta
Recruit initial hosts and users. Controlled reservations. Escrow lifecycle tests. UX iteration. Support workflows.

## Months 7–9 — Public Beta
AI assistant, translation, identity/verification primitives, reputation, disputes, analytics and stablecoin UX.

## Months 10–12 — Validation
Targets: 1,000 registered users, 100 active hosts, 500 listings and USD 50,000 reservation volume. Also public beta, security review, operational AI and on-chain reputation.

## Months 13–15 — Monetization
Test Host Pro at USD 10/month. Measure conversion, retention, willingness to pay and recurring revenue.

## Months 16–18 — Ecosystem
APIs, developer documentation, partner integrations, security bounty, wallet/payment/identity integrations and EVM expansion evaluation.

## Post-18 months
Expansion depends on measured adoption and economics. Potential paths include revenue reinvestment, ecosystem partnerships, additional grants and strategic investment.
