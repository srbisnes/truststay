# TrustStay Grant Proposal

## Request
USD 35,000 for a three-month acceleration phase followed by an 18-month validation and ecosystem-development roadmap.

## Why now
TrustStay already has substantial product design, architecture and conceptual work completed. The grant is intended to compress execution by funding five specialist contributors and the infrastructure, security and deployment work required to reach a functional public beta.

## Team
Five contributors: Web3/Solidity, full-stack, AI, product/UX and growth/ecosystem. Compensation covers three months. The founder retains product ownership and coordinates the roadmap.

## Deliverables
Authentication, wallet connection, listings, search, booking, USDC escrow, reputation foundation, AI foundation, analytics, deployment, testing and security preparation.

## 18-month milestones
Months 1–3: accelerate. Months 4–6: private beta. Months 7–9: public beta. Months 10–12: validation targets. Months 13–15: monetization. Months 16–18: ecosystem.

## Validation targets
1,000 registered users; 100 active hosts; 500 listings; USD 50,000 reservation volume; public beta; security review; operational AI; on-chain reputation. These are targets, not guarantees.

## Sustainability
The grant is catalytic. Growth creates recurring costs for technical maintenance, infrastructure, AI, security, support, compliance, legal and ecosystem development. The intended transition is grant-funded acceleration → validation → revenue → recurring revenue → strategic funding/partnerships.

## Distribution
The project began being shown publicly approximately one month before this funding stage. Distribution will combine build-in-public content, demos, Web3 events, hackathons, community and partner outreach. The founder's Argentine Web3/hackathon network can support contributors, testers and ecosystem relationships.

## EVM strategy
TrustStay is Base-first and EVM-compatible. The long-term goal is interoperable infrastructure for wallets, dApps, payment providers, identity systems, travel communities and other applications.

## Future economics
Any future token or utility NFT layer is conditional on real product usage, clear utility, sustainable economics and legal/security review.
